4 Asking for Directions

This part covers how to ask for directions in English, so you can ask the way when you don't know a place or a route.

Useful phrases

  • Excuse me, how can I get to the hospital?
  • Where is the nearest bank?
  • Could you tell me the way to the train station?
  • Is the supermarket far from here?
  • Which way is the post office?

Example dialogue

A: Excuse me, how can I get to the hospital?
B: Go straight and turn left at the bank. The hospital is on your right.
A: Thank you very much!
