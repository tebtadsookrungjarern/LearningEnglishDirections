5 Giving Directions

This part covers how to give directions in English, so you can guide someone and explain how to travel to different places.

Useful phrases

  • Go straight.
  • Turn left.
  • Turn right.
  • Go past the school.
  • It is next to the bank.
  • Cross the street.
  • You will see it on your right.

Example dialogue

A: How do I get to the library?
B: Go straight, then turn right at the park. Go past the school, and the library is next to the bank.
A: Great, thank you!
